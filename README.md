## Project Overview

As part of my Business Analyst internship at Zoople Technologies, I worked on the Premier Club Website requirements documentation project.

The project involved studying an initial Business Requirements Document (BRD) and translating the business needs into a structured System Requirements Specification (SRS).

The documentation was organised to provide a clear reference for the proposed website's design, development and testing. It covered three major components:

- **Public Website:** Information pages, membership applications, facilities, events and contact features.
- **Member Portal:** Secure login, profile management and professional member directory.
- **Admin Panel:** Content, membership, member, facilities, events and gallery management.

The project helped me gain practical experience in understanding business needs, structuring requirements and documenting system behaviour from the perspectives of different stakeholders.

The BRD and SRS documents are included in this repository for reference.
