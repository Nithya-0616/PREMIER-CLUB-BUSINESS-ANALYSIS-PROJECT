# Premier Club Website – Business Analysis Project

## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of my Business Analyst internship at Zoople Technologies.

The assignment involved analysing the Business Requirements Document (BRD) for a proposed Premier Club Website and translating the high-level business needs into a comprehensive System Requirements Specification (SRS).

The proposed solution consists of three interconnected components:

- Public Website
- Member Portal
- Admin Panel

The primary objective of my work was to understand the business requirements, identify system functionalities, define user interactions, establish acceptance criteria and prepare structured documentation that could serve as a reference for the design, development and testing teams.

## Business Problem

Premier Club required a centralised digital platform to improve its online presence, simplify membership applications, provide secure access to members and support professional networking.

The proposed system needed to address the following business needs:

- Provide visitors with information about the club, facilities, events and membership.
- Enable prospective members to submit membership applications online.
- Allow approved members to securely access and manage their profiles.
- Provide a searchable professional member directory.
- Enable administrators to manage website content, members, memberships, facilities, events and gallery information.

## Business Analysis Activities

The project involved translating business expectations into detailed and testable system requirements.

### Requirements Analysis

- Studied the supplied Business Requirements Document.
- Identified business objectives, stakeholders and project scope.
- Analysed functional requirements across the three system components.
- Identified assumptions, dependencies, constraints and out-of-scope features.
- Expanded high-level requirements into detailed system-level requirements.

### User Story Development

Prepared user stories from the perspectives of different system users, including visitors, prospective members, existing members and administrators.

Each user story included:

- Story ID
- User story name
- Related business requirements
- Priority
- User role
- User requirement and business value
- Pre-conditions
- Acceptance criteria
- Alternate flows
- Post-conditions

### Acceptance Criteria

Defined clear and testable acceptance criteria to describe the expected behaviour of individual system functionalities.

Alternate flows were also documented to address scenarios such as invalid inputs, duplicate applications, unavailable services and unsuccessful submissions.

### Business Rules

Identified and documented business rules governing membership approval, account activation, access permissions, content publication, member privacy and administrative operations.

### Process Modelling

Prepared process models to explain system interactions and workflows:

- Use Case Diagram
- Swimlane Diagram
- Process Flowchart

### Non-Functional Requirements

Documented quality requirements covering:

- Security and authentication
- Role-based access
- Performance
- Usability
- Browser compatibility
- Responsive design
- Accessibility
- System reliability

### Requirements Traceability

Prepared requirements traceability matrices connecting business requirements with corresponding user stories, business rules and acceptance test scenarios.

This helped establish a structured relationship between the original business needs and the proposed system behaviour.

## Important Business Rules and Scenarios

Some of the business scenarios addressed in the SRS included:

- Only approved members can activate their member portal accounts.
- Membership applications require administrative review and approval.
- Duplicate applications are checked using the applicant's email address.
- Only published website content is visible to public visitors.
- Administrators can access modules based on their assigned permissions.
- Member information displayed in the directory must respect privacy settings.
